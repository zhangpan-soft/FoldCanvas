# Community conduct

FoldCanvas is an engineering and research community. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.

- Critique ideas, code, tests, and claims rather than people.
- Distinguish verified behavior from hypotheses.
- Welcome contributors who do not have traditional 3D-modeling experience.
- Do not gatekeep through unexplained jargon.
- Share reproducible cases when reporting geometry failures.
- Respect licenses, attribution, and private security reports.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted. Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
